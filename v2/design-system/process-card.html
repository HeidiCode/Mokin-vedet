Component

Process card

One card per guide on the home view. The whole card is a button that opens the process, and its counter shows how many steps are done.

Best practices

Do

  • Name the process by season and direction: Kesä — Palaaminen.
  • Keep the whole card one button.

Don't

  • Put a second button or a link inside the card.
  • Let the counter's width follow the digits; it would land on half pixels.

Accessibility

  • It is a real <button>, so it works with keyboard and screen readers.
  • The counter is read as part of the button's name. A hidden "vaihetta valmiina" makes it "Kesä — Palaaminen 0/5 vaihetta valmiina".
  • Coming back from a process, focus returns to the card you opened.
