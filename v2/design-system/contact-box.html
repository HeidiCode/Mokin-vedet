Component

Contact box

A quiet box pinned to the bottom of the home view that tells you who to call if something goes wrong. It has no shadow because it is not a control.

Anatomy

ClassRole
.contact-boxWhite box with a 2px ink border and no shadow. Pushed to the bottom of the home view.
.contact-box__titleShort question in Space Grotesk.
.contact-box__textOne line of body text.
.iconThe info icon, in --accent-ink.

Best practices

Do

  • Keep it to a question and a name.
  • Use it once, on the home view.

Don't

  • Give it a shadow; that would make it look pressable.
  • Turn it into an error or alert box.

Accessibility

  • The icon is decorative; the text says everything.
