Primary button

The one action on screen. It is full width, filled with the accent, and casts a hard shadow that it presses into when tapped. It appears on the current step and in the completion modal.

Examples

Anatomy

ClassRole
.btn-completeFull-width accent button on the current step, inside .step-actions.
.modal__btnThe same button inside the completion modal.
:activeMoves 3px down and right into its shadow, which disappears.

Best practices

Do

  • Write the action out as a verb: Merkitse valmiiksi.
  • Use one primary button per view.

Don't

  • Use an icon on its own; testers did not understand the icon-only modal button.
  • Put two primary buttons side by side.

Accessibility

  • It is a real <button type="button">.
  • The press animation is switched off under reduced motion.
  • Dark text on the accent fill passes 4.5:1.
