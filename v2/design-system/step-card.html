Component

Step card

The heart of the app. A process is a straight line of steps and exactly one is current. The step card shows each step in one of three states, and only the current one asks for attention.

Best practices

Do

  • Write the title as an instruction: verb first, one action.
  • Keep exactly one current step.
  • Let people rewind by tapping a done step.

Don't

  • Show the body of a locked step; it would invite skipping ahead.
  • Tell states apart by colour alone; background, border, badge and the open body all change together.
  • Make the current card's border thicker.

Accessibility

  • Valmis and Kesken are hidden visually but read out, so the state is never only visual.
  • The padlock is an image with aria-label="Lukittu".
  • When a step is marked done or rewound, focus moves to the new current step's head, which has tabindex="-1" for that reason, and a hidden live region announces the change.
  • The locked card's dashed border is its only outline and stays above 3:1.
