Component

Note (Huomio)

A warning inside a step: something that must not be missed, such as not powering a dry boiler. It is a note to read, not an alarm. The label rides the top border as a tab.

Examples

Huomio

Kytke virta vasta kun olet varmistanut että vesi tulee. Kuiva boileri ei saa olla virrallinen.

Anatomy

ClassRole
.alert-boxPale yellow box with a 2px ink border and extra top padding for the tab.
.alert-box__titleThe Huomio tab, centred on the top border. White uppercase text on --accent-ink.
.alert-box__textThe warning, as body text.
.iconWarning glyph, kept in the markup but hidden; the tab already says it.

Best practices

Do

  • Say what to do and why, in one or two sentences.
  • Place it after the instructions it qualifies.

Don't

  • Use red, or style it as an error.
  • Use more than one note in a step.

Accessibility

  • The label is real text, so "Huomio" is read before the warning.
  • White on --accent-ink clears 4.5:1.
