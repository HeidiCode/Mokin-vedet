Rules

  • Labels are uppercase Manrope at weight 800 with 0.06em letter-spacing, in --text-accent.
  • Line heights are written in ems and snapped to whole pixels: line-height: round(1.5em, 1px). They scale with the text but never land between pixels.
  • Headings tighten slightly: letter-spacing: -0.02em on the 25px heading, -0.01em in the app bar.

Best practices

Do

  • Pick a size from the scale with its token.
  • Keep instructions short and in plain Finnish.
  • Use a label above a block of text to name it: Ohjeet, Työkalut, Liitteet.

Don't

  • Go below 14px, even for small print.
  • Add a third font family.
  • Use uppercase for anything longer than a label.
