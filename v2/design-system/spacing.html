Foundations

Spacing and shape

Everything sits on an 8px grid, every line is 2px, and every edge lands on a whole pixel. Cards are softly rounded and cast a hard ink shadow when they can be pressed.

Spacing scale

Use 8, 16 and 24 for almost everything. 4px is reserved for the gap between a label and its text, and for the inset of a pill or counter. Alike elements get the same value.

Borders

--border is 2px, on every card, button, badge and box, including the current step and the modal. State is never shown with a thicker line. The locked step is the one exception in style, not width: its border is dashed.

Shadow

A hard, unblurred shadow offset by --shadow-offset marks something you can press. On press, the element moves into its shadow. The current step and the modal cast a wider shadow in the accent colour instead.

3px inkButtons, process cards
5px accentCurrent step
noneDone and locked steps, static boxes

Pixel precision

  • No fractional widths or positions: no 1.5px lines, no half-pixel offsets.
  • A box whose width follows its text, like the 3/12 counter, gets a fixed min-width so its border never falls between two pixels.
  • Measure in a browser, not only in the CSS.
