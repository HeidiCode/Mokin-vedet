Foundations

Colour

A pale green page with white cards floating on it, dark lines for structure, and one soft lavender accent for the thing to press. There is no red: nothing in this app is an error.

Every colour is named by its role first, then a qualifier: bg- fills an area, text- is read, border and shadow draw structure, icon- is a glyph. Pick the token by what you are painting, not by its hex.

Background

The page is pale green. Cards sit on it in white. A step that is done, or not reached yet, recedes into a green tint so the current white card stands out.

Text

--text is everything you read. --text-muted is the locked step, which recedes. --text-accent is for small uppercase labels, which need more contrast than the accent fill can give.

Border and shadow

--border draws every line and fills the progress bar; --shadow is every hard drop shadow. Today both follow --text, so one dark shade carries the whole page, but each can move on its own.

Icon, accent and overlay

--accent is a fill that always has dark text on top. --overlay darkens the page behind the lightbox and the completion modal.

Best practices

Do

  • Use a token for every colour: var(--bg-card), never #ffffff.
  • Show state with background, shadow and badge together.
  • Put dark text on --accent; use --text-accent for labels.
  • Check contrast on the accessibility page after any retint.

Don't

  • Dim anything with opacity. It drops text below 4.5:1.
  • Add red or other alarm colours for warnings.
  • Use --accent as a text colour.
  • Let colour be the only thing that tells two states apart.
