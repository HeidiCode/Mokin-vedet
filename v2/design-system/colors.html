Foundations

Colour

A pale green page with white cards floating on it, ink for every line, and one soft lavender accent for the thing to press. There is no red: nothing in this app is an error.

Surfaces

The ground is the page. Cards sit on it in white. A step that is done, or not reached yet, recedes into a green tint so the current white card stands out.

Ink and text

--ink is structure: borders, shadows, the progress fill. --text is what you read. Today they are the same colour under two names, so either can move without the other.

Accent

Two accents that do not follow each other. --accent is a fill that always has dark text on top. --accent-ink is a text colour for small uppercase labels, which need more contrast than a fill.

Best practices

Do

  • Use a token for every colour: var(--card), never #ffffff.
  • Show state with background, shadow and badge together.
  • Put dark text on --accent; use --accent-ink for labels.
  • Check contrast on the accessibility page after any retint.

Don't

  • Dim anything with opacity. It drops text below 4.5:1.
  • Add red or other alarm colours for warnings.
  • Use --accent as a text colour.
  • Let colour be the only thing that tells two states apart.
