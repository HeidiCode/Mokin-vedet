Component

Photo and lightbox

Steps show the valve or switch to turn as photos. Each thumbnail carries an ink AVAA bar so it reads as something to open; opening it shows the photo full screen.

Best practices

Do

  • Crop photos so the part to touch is in the middle.
  • Write an alt text for every photo, in Finnish.
  • Reuse one photo across steps through its key in PHOTOS.

Don't

  • Remove the AVAA bar; testers did not realise photos opened without it.
  • Put text you need to read only inside a photo.

Accessibility

  • Each thumbnail is a button labelled "Avaa kuva N".
  • The lightbox is a dialog: it takes focus on its close button, keeps Tab inside and returns focus to the thumbnail that opened it.
  • Escape closes the lightbox. The focus ring turns white on the dark overlay.
