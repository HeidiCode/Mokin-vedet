Component

App bar

The white bar across the top of every view. It holds the page title, which is the page's <h1>, and inside a process a back button to the home view.

Best practices

Do

  • Use the process name as the title inside a process.
  • Keep the back button the only control in the bar.

Don't

  • Add actions or a menu to the bar; the app has one task per view.
  • Let a long title wrap to two lines; it truncates.

Accessibility

  • The title is the page's only <h1>.
  • The back button has aria-label="Takaisin" because it shows only an icon.
  • The button is 44 × 44px, the minimum touch target.
