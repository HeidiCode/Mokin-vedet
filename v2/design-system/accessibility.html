Foundations

Accessibility

Metsä targets WCAG 2.1 AA and is checked with axe-core. The ratios below are computed in your browser from the live tokens, so a retint that breaks one shows up here straight away.

Contrast

Text needs 4.5:1. A boundary that is the only outline of something, like the locked card's dashed border, needs 3:1, and so does an icon that carries meaning.

Never dim with opacity

Opacity lowers contrast unpredictably and almost always pushes text below 4.5:1. A muted element gets an explicit colour token instead: the locked step uses --muted on --locked-bg.

Focus

Keyboard focus is a 2px --ink ring with a 2px gap, so it reads on every surface. Inside the lightbox the ring is white. A done step's whole card is ringed, not just its head.

Checklist

  • Language. <html lang="fi"> in the app.
  • Headings. The page title is the <h1>; phase labels are <h2>.
  • State in words. The words Valmis and Kesken are hidden visually with .sr-only but still read out. The padlock has aria-label="Lukittu".
  • Photos. Each thumbnail button has a numbered label, "Avaa kuva 1", and its image an alt text.
  • Overlays. The lightbox and the modal are role="dialog" with aria-modal; they take focus when they open, keep Tab inside, and give focus back when they close.
  • Touch targets. At least 44 × 44px.
  • Zoom. The viewport must not set maximum-scale.
  • Motion. Transitions and smooth scrolling are switched off under prefers-reduced-motion.

Re-run axe-core against the home view, a process and the completion modal after any visual change.
